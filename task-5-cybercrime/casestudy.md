# Case Study: UPI Payment Fraud

UPI payment fraud is a common cybercrime where attackers trick users into sending money or sharing sensitive information. This type of fraud usually happens through fake calls, messages, or links.

In a typical scenario, a fraudster pretends to be a bank official or a seller and contacts the victim. They may send a fake payment request or ask the user to scan a QR code, claiming that money will be received. However, instead of receiving money, the victim unknowingly approves a payment and loses funds.

Students and individuals who are new to digital payments are often targeted because they may not be fully aware of such scams. The consequences include financial loss, stress, and sometimes misuse of personal information.

Such frauds are increasing due to the widespread use of digital payment apps. It is important for users to understand how these scams work so they can avoid falling victim to them.
