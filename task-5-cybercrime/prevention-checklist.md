# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

- Never share your UPI PIN with anyone  
- Do not scan QR codes to receive money  
- Always verify payment requests before approving  
- Avoid clicking on unknown or suspicious links  
- Use strong passwords for all accounts  
- Enable two-factor authentication where possible  
- Regularly check your bank transaction history  
- Do not share OTPs with anyone  
- Be cautious of calls claiming to be from banks  
- Report fraud immediately on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
